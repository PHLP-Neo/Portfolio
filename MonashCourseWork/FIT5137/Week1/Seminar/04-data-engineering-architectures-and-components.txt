Data Engineering Components
1. Databases
- Based on a simple model: relational model (tables, data integrity)
- Transaction Management (DB vs. File Systems)
- ACID Transactions (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability)
2. Data Warehouse
- Precomputed values
- Granularity
- Also based on relational technology
- But must pre-designed
